Allotments win grant for rainwater store

The Millbrook allotment society has won a 12,000-pound grant to build a rainwater store for its 140 plots on Green Lane.

The money comes from a regional fund for community food projects and will pay for four large tanks, guttering on the two sheds and a hand pump.

Plot holders have relied on mains taps that were switched off for six weeks during last summer's hosepipe ban, and several crops failed.

The society's secretary said the store should hold about 30,000 litres, enough to keep most plots watered through a dry month.
