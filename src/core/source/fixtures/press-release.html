Press release

Council announces new recycling service

Millbrook Council will start collecting food waste from every household from April. The service is free and will be introduced street by street over six months.

Residents will receive a caddy and a leaflet explaining what can go in it. The council expects the scheme to cut general waste by about a fifth.

Full details, including the collection calendar, are in the council's briefing paper, which is available as a PDF.
