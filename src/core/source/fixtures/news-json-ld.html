Bridge reopens after repairs

The Old Mill footbridge reopened on Tuesday after fourteen weeks of repairs, the council said. Engineers replaced the deck and repainted the steelwork.

The council's own report on the work is published in full, and the independent inspector's findings are on the government's website for anyone to read.

Read the inspection report on gov.uk and the engineering paper it cites.

Traders on Mill Lane said the closure had cost them trade, and some said they would welcome a review of how the council plans its repair programme.
