Why the high street will not come back

Councils keep promising that shoppers will return to the high street if they are given more parking and new benches. The evidence says otherwise, and the sooner we admit it, the better.

The national statistics office publishes footfall figures every month, and they show that shoppers have moved online for good. A new bench will not change that.

My argument is that the money should go to housing above the shops and to the bus services that bring people into town, rather than to another round of consultations.
