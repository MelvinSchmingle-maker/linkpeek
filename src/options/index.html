LinkPeek settings

Where LinkPeek runs by itself

In Smart mode, LinkPeek adds icons on sites you've turned on. Everywhere else it only runs when you click it, right-click a link, or use the shortcut.

      Summaries

      Summaries are always 20 words or fewer, and the card always says who wrote them. If the one you choose can't, LinkPeek picks a sentence from the article instead.

      Link warnings you turned off

      A warning about a link's address or text (Part 11) can be switched off for that link's site with "Don't warn me on this site". The check still runs. Remove a site here to see its warnings again.

        Annoyance tools

        Off unless you turn them on. These are the choices for every site. The popup can change them for one site. They work on pages where LinkPeek runs, and the tools are separate: each one can be switched on alone.

          Your feedback

          Votes you've given on this device: thumbs up and down on summaries, and "Disagree with this rating?" on headline checks. Nothing is sent anywhere.

            Your data

            Nothing goes to LinkPeek or anyone else. When you open a card, LinkPeek fetches that one page from its own website, without your cookies. Settings, your votes (with any reasons you gave) and recent results stay in your browser.
