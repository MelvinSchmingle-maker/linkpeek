Where LinkPeek runs by itself

In Smart mode, LinkPeek adds icons on sites you've turned on. Everywhere else it only runs when you click it, right-click a link, or use the shortcut.

      Summaries

      LinkPeek's summaries are always 20 words or fewer. By default it picks the article's most informative sentence, and the card says "In the article's own words".

      Keyboard shortcut

      Peek at the focused link: Alt+Shift+P

      Change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

      Your data

      Nothing goes to LinkPeek or anyone else. When you open a card, LinkPeek fetches that one page from its own website, without your cookies. Settings, your thumbs up/down votes and recent results stay in your browser.
