What I learned growing tomatoes on a balcony

12 Sep 2026

I have grown tomatoes on a small balcony for three summers now, and the first two were mostly a lesson in what not to do with a south-facing wall.

The first year I planted six varieties in shallow troughs, watered them whenever I remembered, and harvested about a dozen split fruit in late September.

The second year I moved to deep pots, set up a simple drip line from a water butt, and still lost most of the crop to blight during a wet August.

This year I grew only two varieties that are known to resist blight, kept the leaves off the ground, and pinched out the side shoots every weekend.

The difference was obvious by July: steady growth, no yellowing leaves, and more fruit than we could eat between us and the neighbours downstairs.

If you are starting out, my advice is to pick fewer plants, give them deeper containers than you think they need, and water at the same time every day.
