Footbridge reopens after summer rebuild

20:13, 17 Oct 2026Updated 21:01, 17 Oct 2026

Council crews praised for finishing the rebuild ahead of the autumn term

The Old Mill footbridge reopened on Tuesday morning, a little over three months after it was closed for rebuilding.

Engineers spent the summer replacing the deck, which had been patched so often that nobody could say how much of the original timber was left.

The work ran three weeks late after a delivery of steel brackets arrived in the wrong size and had to be sent back to the supplier in Leeds.

Shopkeepers on Mill Lane said trade fell by about a third while the crossing was shut, because people from the estate stopped walking into town.

One cafe owner said she had kept a tally of customers on a sheet behind the till and would be sending it to the council with a bill.

The council said it would not pay compensation but would waive market stall fees for traders on the lane until the end of the year.

A second phase, adding lighting and a ramp at the eastern end, is planned for spring, subject to a funding bid that has not yet been decided.
