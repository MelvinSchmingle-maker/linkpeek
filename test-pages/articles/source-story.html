Footbridge reopens after repairs

The Old Mill footbridge reopened on Tuesday after fourteen weeks of repairs, the council said. Engineers replaced the deck and repainted the steelwork.

The full condition report is available as a PDF, and the government's own guidance on footbridge inspections is published on gov.uk for anyone to read.

Read the footbridge condition report (PDF) and the inspection guidance.

Traders on Mill Lane said the closure had cost them trade, and the council said it would review how repairs are planned next year.
