Three ways to cut your heating bill

Heating bills are still high, and many households are looking for ways to spend less this winter without giving up warmth in the living room.

This guide is paid for by a boiler company. It recommends a service in October and a smart thermostat, and says the savings are typical rather than guaranteed.

Another way to cut costs is to check whether your home qualifies for insulation grants, which are listed on the government's website.
