Library review recommends changes to opening hours

A review of Millbrook's library service has recommended longer opening hours at weekends and a new mobile library for the villages north of the river.

The review, carried out over the summer, found that visits fell by a third on weekdays but stayed steady on Saturdays, when the main library closes at one o'clock.

It suggests opening until five on Saturdays and adding Sunday afternoon hours during school holidays, paid for by closing an hour earlier on Monday and Tuesday evenings.

Staff told the reviewers that the evening sessions are mostly used by people printing documents, and that a self-service printer could cover most of that demand.

The mobile library would visit Ashby, Long Fen and three smaller villages every fortnight, using a converted van that the county already owns.

Councillors will discuss the recommendations next month, and the library will run a consultation in all branches until the end of November.

Residents can also comment online or by post, and the review team will hold two drop-in sessions at the main library in early November.
